# Surface: employee business card (index.html / src/App.jsx)

Mode: Persuade (the visitor acts: save contact, call, message). Audience: someone who just met the employee, on a phone, seconds after a handshake. Action: Save Contact first, then Call/WhatsApp/Email/LinkedIn, then company website/location. Constraints: exactly two screens, no intro splash, data in src/config.js.

Direction note: the user pinned the reference video (HILF Shipping card) as the visual and interaction world, so no concept roll was run; a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Translated into YOP brand material.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A ledger card. The phone becomes a dark plum business card whose only job is getting one person into your contacts; it refuses the portfolio-page arrangement (hero, about, skills, sections).

OWN-WORLD: Near-black aubergine ground (#170a13) drawn from brand plum #65063C; bone-white type; pill buttons, one white-filled primary; contact rows as 18px-radius slabs with square icon tiles; a ruler of ledger ticks as the seam into a cool off-white company screen where plum carries the primary pill and the logo.

STORY: Visitor sees the name, knows who and what, taps Save Contact, the native contact sheet opens; the diamond-check mark ticks to confirm. Scroll: the ruler, then who Your Office Partners is, with website and map.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered column max 26rem. Name at clamp(2.5rem, 11vw, 3.75rem) Archivo 800 tight; role below; mark + YOUR OFFICE PARTNERS tracked caps. Two equal pills: Save contact (white, primary) and Call. Contact list label with hairline, three rows (WhatsApp with number, Email, LinkedIn) whose brand-color fill sweeps in on hover/press. Ruler top edge visible at the fold as a scroll cue.

FORM: Pinned reference (user-supplied video); position n/a; seed key: none (pinned, roll skipped).

Signature interaction: Save Contact tap draws the logo's check mark inside the button and shows a platform-specific hint; ruler ticks rise in a wave that travels with scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
